## 5. Key Discussions

Discussion 1

Topic: UI Mockup Complexity & Usability

Summary: The student team presented a high-level mock interface featuring multi-panel video navigation, transcript panes, and engagement questions. The client team observed that the interface looked like a dense academic platform that would overwhelm new volunteer tutors and parents. They emphasized that the platform must be layperson-friendly and focus on simple, stepwise navigation.

Outcome: The student team agreed to simplify the layout, replacing dense multi-panel views with a clean, stepwise workflow and clear visual cues.

Discussion 2

Topic: Prototyping Platform & Asynchronous Approvals

Summary: The team discussed whether to build the revised low-fidelity prototype in Figma or Canva and how the client approval workflow would operate.

Outcome: Agreed to build the simplified stepwise prototype in Canva and share it with the client team for internal review and asynchronous sign-off prior to frontend implementation.

Discussion 3

Topic: Curriculum Assets and Tag Taxonomy Supply

Summary: The student team requested guidance on the exact tag keywords, icons, and training materials used by IMLC. Prof. Sridhar and Farah confirmed that curriculum templates and tag definitions will be shared once the simplified screen flow is established.

Outcome: The student team will review existing IMLC assets and coordinate with Farah and Jamson to align on tag keywords and sample training content.
